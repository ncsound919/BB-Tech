## SLIDE 5: HOW IT WORKS — Example

### Headline:
**"Case Study: Steph Curry → Viral Infection Modeling"**

### The Process:

**Step 1: Identify pattern**
- Steph Curry's 3-point shooting revolutionized NBA
- Spread from Warriors → league-wide adoption
- Defensive schemes evolved in response

**Step 2: Extract metrics**
- Propagation rate: shots per game increase across league
- Adaptation response: defensive coverage adjustments
- Saturation point: when strategy becomes baseline

**Step 3: Translate to biology**
- Viral infection rate (R0)
- Host immune response kinetics
- Endemic equilibrium

**Step 4: Generate testable hypotheses**
- Predict infection spread patterns
- Model resistance emergence
- Design intervention strategies
