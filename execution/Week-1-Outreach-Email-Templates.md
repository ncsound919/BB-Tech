## EMAIL BEST PRACTICES

### Timing
- **Send:** Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am or 2-4pm EST
- **Follow-up 1:** +5 business days
- **Follow-up 2:** +7 business days after follow-up 1
- **Maximum:** 3 touchpoints before pausing outreach

### Personalization Checklist
- [ ] Recipient name and title
- [ ] Specific reference to their recent work/publication
- [ ] Relevant BBTech archetype/model to their research area
- [ ] Customized "why now" rationale
- [ ] Clear, single call-to-action

### Attachments (When Referenced)
1. **BBTech One-Pager** (1 page PDF)
   - Platform overview
   - Key archetypes
   - Early validation metrics
   - Team credentials

2. **Methodology Deck** (8-10 slides)
   - Translation framework
   - Example: Steph Curry → Virus model
   - Validation approach
   - Partnership structures

3. **Validation Data** (2-3 pages)
   - Preliminary results
   - Statistical significance
   - Next steps for full validation

### Subject Line Rules
- **Length:** 6-8 words max
- **Avoid:** "Collaboration", "Opportunity" (overused)
- **Include:** Specificity (player name, disease type, methodology)
- **Test:** Would YOU open this cold email?

### Response Handling

**If interested:**
→ Schedule call within 48 hours
→ Send calendar invite with agenda
→ Prepare customized pitch deck

**If not interested:**
→ Thank them for responding
→ Ask for 6-month check-in permission
→ Request referral to colleague if appropriate

**If no response after 3 touchpoints:**
→ Add to "nurture" list
→ Quarterly update emails with new validation data
→ Monitor their publications for re-engagement triggers

## CUSTOMIZATION GUIDE BY TARGET TYPE

### Academic (Cancer Centers, Universities)
- **Lead with:** Scientific novelty, publication opportunity
- **Emphasize:** No-cost pilot, co-authorship, grant applications
- **Tone:** Collaborative, peer-to-peer, intellectually curious

### Biotech (Small-Mid Size Companies)
- **Lead with:** Competitive advantage, novel approach to hard problems
- **Emphasize:** Speed to validation, complementary capabilities
- **Tone:** Partnership-oriented, pragmatic, results-focused

### Pharma (Large Companies)
- **Lead with:** Innovation, alternative data sources, risk mitigation
- **Emphasize:** Pilot program, measurable milestones, scalability
- **Tone:** Professional, structured, business-case driven

### Digital Health / Informatics
- **Lead with:** Platform integration, real-time capabilities, clinical utility
- **Emphasize:** Technical compatibility, user experience, regulatory pathway
- **Tone:** Product-focused, technical, implementation-ready
